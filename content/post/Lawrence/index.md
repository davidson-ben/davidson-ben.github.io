Thanks to Relena Ribbons and the Lawrence University Department of Geosciences for hosting me on campus for an invited lecture. I had a great time presenting my dissertation work regarding beach plastic transport and fate to the senior capstone class.

I also had the privilege of listening to the students share their 30-second elevator pitches for their senior thesis projects. I shared some tips and techniques that I apply in my own science communication and my experiences navigating graduate school as a scientist. It was impressive to see the caliber and variety of research projects these students have been pursuing!
