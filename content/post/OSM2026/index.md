Intro about attending OSM in Glasgow and my exciting vacation ahead of time.

Attended a great Sci Policy session - one of the takeaways was writing a blog or podcast.  I'm not going to commit to publishing a full on BLOG, but I'll be a bit more active in posting what I am up to in my research and work and also in my work to stay up to date with various things in the world of environmental fluid mechanics and especially related to Great Lakes current issues.  Really, I will write about whatever interests me and should anyone else be interested in my thoughts, research, or whatever, I will post it on my website and for now will be sharing my posts with my linked in connections.

Please feel free to reach out for further conversations or to engage further, I hope this is an opportunity to work to bring not only my science, but also other relevant novel research out to others who may find it interesting.  My goal is that well informed citizens can be informed voters and informed communicators to their legislators concerning topical issues.  Scientists can remain objective while also being advocates, in fact, I believe that scientists have a duty to be advocates and this is my way of extending the reach of my advocacy.

So, not sure how this will end up, how often I'll post, how well I'll keep up with this especially as I work to wrap up my dissertation research, but I do think that this first post is a chance to put myself out here and at least not be totally new if I decide to write more about the things that interest me and are of concern in the global, local, and regional water resources environments.
